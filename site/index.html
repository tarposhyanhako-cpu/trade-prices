How we calculate it. Average price = total value ÷ total quantity over the chosen period, from monthly data that Armenia reports to UN Comtrade. Price per kg uses net weight. Price per unit uses the product's own unit (litres, pieces and so on) when Comtrade gives one. Rows with a unit value more than 10 times above or below the product's median are left out of the price, because they are usually recording errors.
